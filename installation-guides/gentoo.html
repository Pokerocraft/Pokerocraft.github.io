Beginner Install of Gentoo

Gentoo is probably known for the jokes about taking a while to compile, which I could come up with a joke about it, but it will take millenia to compile such a joke.

Now, generally it's recommended to specifically read the Gentoo wiki, though I would assume like the Arch Wiki, it may be a lot to take in, that's why I decided to write this guide.

One thing I think you would assume is that with Gentoo that you have to compile everything from source, but no, Gentoo has binary packages, so you don't have to spend actual hours and or days installing KDE Plasma.

I will say that Gentoo does support quite a few architectures, as the official download page does have amd64 (x86_64), arm64 (64-bit ARM), alpha, arm, hppa, loong, mips, m68k, ppc, riscv, s390, sparc, and x86, though the one we'll be tackling is amd64.
