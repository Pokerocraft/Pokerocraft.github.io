Battle System

The Battle System I would say was kind of a unique-ish battle system inspired by Undertale/Deltarune's Battle System, you could attack foes, try and use actions to have them stop attacking you, items, sparing them, a special ability, and fleeing.
Aside from fighting and the special ability, everything works similarly to Undertale I guess, so I don't think I need to explain those, the one I would explain would be fighting, where rather than some bar sliding, you are instead put into a box where by pressing your interacting button (Which on Keyboard Controls is Z), you would be able to make a pattern of sorts that foes had to dodge, similar to the player in Undertale or Deltarune.
I will also mention that rather than having HP where you get a game over when it's fully depleted, you instead have stamina, where once all party members are downed, you wind up at an area's inn. I think I made the concept a while ago, but struggled with it and was considering making it either Deltarune based or based on a generic RPG.
